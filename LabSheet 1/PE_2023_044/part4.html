Network Topologies

Physical topology refers to the way in which a network is laid out physically. Common geometrical arrangements include Mesh, Star, Bus, and Ring topologies.

Transmission Modes

Data transmission between two devices can occur in three modes: Simplex (unidirectional), Half-Duplex (bidirectional alternately), and Full-Duplex (simultaneous bidirectional).
