Fleet & Marine Sanctuary Terminology

Amphibious Seaplan
An aircraft equipped with specialized pontoons/floats enabling takeoffs and landings on both water surfaces and paved airport runways.
Biosphere Reserve
Protected terrestrial and coastal ecosystems certified for sustainable coexistence, clean carbon-neutral tourism, and wildlife conservation.
